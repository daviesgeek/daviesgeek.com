## Everything you need to understand your health

### Custom Dashboard

**Build your personal health dashboard**

Create widgets for the metrics you care about most. Drag and drop to organize your dashboard exactly how you want it. See your health story at a glance.

### 100+ Health Metrics

**Track everything Apple Health records**

Steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, blood pressure, nutrition, and so much more. If Apple Health tracks it, you can chart it. Over 100 metrics supported.

### Beautiful Charts

**Seven chart types for every need**

Bar charts, line graphs, area charts, scatter plots, pie charts, threshold lines, and simple text displays. Pick the perfect visualization for each metric.

### Data Explorer

**Dive deep into your raw data**

Browse individual health samples. Filter by date and category. See exactly where each measurement came from. Your data, fully transparent.

### Privacy First

**Your data stays on your device**

We never collect, store, or share your health information. Health Charts reads directly from Apple Health, and that's where your data stays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What health data can I track?**

Health Charts supports over 100 metrics from Apple HealthKit, including steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, blood pressure, blood glucose, nutrition data, and more. If Apple Health stores it, you can visualize it.

**Is my health data safe?**

Absolutely. Your health data never leaves your device. Health Charts reads directly from Apple Health and doesn't upload, store, or share your personal health information with anyone.

**Do I need an Apple Watch?**

No! Health Charts works with any data in Apple Health. This includes data from your iPhone, Apple Watch, third-party fitness trackers, smart scales, blood pressure monitors, and health apps.

**What's included in the free version?**

The free tier includes 2 custom widgets, 3 chart types (plain text, bar chart, and line chart), access to the Data Explorer, and support for all 100+ health metrics.

**Can I try Premium before subscribing?**

Yes! Every new user gets a 7-day free trial of Premium with full access to unlimited widgets, all chart types, and customization options. Cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged.

**What iOS version do I need?**

Health Charts requires iOS 18 or later.
