# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 12, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

Weekly Picks does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information or photos. All data remains on your device.

## 2. Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your Photo Library solely to read photo metadata (creation dates) and organize photos into albums. Your photos are never uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

## 3. Local Storage

App preferences (such as week start day and parent album name) are stored locally on your device. This data is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## 4. Data Security

Since all processing occurs locally on your device and no data is transmitted, your photos and information remain secure within iOS's built-in security features.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Weekly Picks does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app operates entirely offline.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

## 7. Your Rights

You can revoke photo library access at any time through iOS Settings. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app. Albums created by the app will remain in your Photo Library.

## 8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact through the App Store.

## Summary

In short: Your photos stay on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. The app simply helps you organize your existing photos into weekly albums locally.
