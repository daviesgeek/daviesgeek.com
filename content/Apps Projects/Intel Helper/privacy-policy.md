## Introduction

Intel Helper is an iOS application developed by Matthew Davies (daviesgeek) that provides an enhanced interface for the Ingress Intel Map. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

**Our Privacy Commitment:** Intel Helper is designed with a local-first approach. Your data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored exclusively on your device and is **never transmitted to us**:

- **Bookmarks:** Saved Intel map locations and associated names
- **Map State:** Your last viewed map position (latitude, longitude, zoom level)
- **App Preferences:** In-app purchase status, and other app configuration
- **HTTP Cookies:** Map position data used by the Ingress Intel Map web interface
- **Persistent Storage:** App data managed through iOS Core Data

This information remains under your control and can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing app data through iOS Settings.

### Information Shared with Third Parties

Intel Helper integrates with several third-party services that may collect information directly from you:

1. **Crash Reports (Crashlytics/Firebase)**

   - Device model and iOS version
   - Crash logs and diagnostic data
   - App version and usage patterns at time of crash
   - This data helps us identify and fix bugs to improve app stability

2. **Advertising Data (Google AdMob)** - only if you haven't purchased Pro
   - Device advertising identifier (IDFA)
   - Device type and operating system
   - App usage for ad targeting
   - Subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework
   - Removed completely when you purchase the "Pro" upgrade

### Location Data

Intel Helper requests access to your device location to:

- Center the Intel map on your current position
- Enable location-based map features

**Important:** Your location data is used **only locally** on your device to interact with the Ingress Intel Map. We do not transmit, collect, or store your location data on our servers.

---

## What We Don't Collect

We want to be clear about what we **do not** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- User-generated content beyond local bookmarks
- Contact lists, calendar data, or health information
- Photos or media library access
- Precise location tracking or location history
- Payment information (handled entirely by Apple)
- Analytics data beyond crash reports
- Search history or browsing behavior

We use crash report data to:

We **do not**:

- Sell or rent your information to third parties
- Use your data for marketing purposes

## Third-Party Services

Intel Helper relies on several third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

### 1. Ingress Intel Map (Niantic)

The core functionality of Intel Helper loads the official Ingress Intel Map from Niantic in a web view. When you use the Intel map:

- You are subject to [Niantic's Privacy Policy](https://nianticlabs.com/privacy/)
- You must authenticate with your Google account through Niantic's systems
- The Intel map may receive your location data if you choose to share it
- We have no control over Niantic's data collection or practices

**Disclaimer:** Intel Helper is an independent, unofficial application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Niantic, Inc. or the Ingress game.

### 2. Google AdMob (Advertising)

If you use the free version of Intel Helper, we display advertisements through Google AdMob:

- AdMob collects device identifiers for ad personalization
- You can limit ad tracking through iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking
- Ads are removed when you purchase the "Pro" upgrade
- Learn more: [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### 3. Crashlytics/Firebase (Crash Reporting)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports:

- Helps us identify and fix app crashes
- Collects anonymous diagnostic data
- Learn more: [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### 4. Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple:

- We receive only confirmation of purchase status
- Payment information is handled by Apple, not us
- Subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

Your bookmarks, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using:

- iOS UserDefaults
- Core Data persistent storage
- HTTP cookies managed by iOS

This data is protected by your device's security features, including device encryption and iOS sandbox protections.

### Server Storage

**We do not operate servers that store your personal data.** The app communicates directly with third-party services as described above.

### Data Retention

- Local data persists until you delete the app or clear app data
- Crash reports are retained by Firebase according to their retention policies
- Purchase records are maintained by Apple

## Your Privacy Rights

### California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **Know:** Request information about data collected (we don't collect personal data on our servers)
- **Delete:** Request deletion of your data (uninstall the app to remove all local data)
- **Opt-Out:** Opt out of data "sales" (we don't sell your data)

To exercise these rights, contact us at intelhelper@daviesgeek.com.

### European Users (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability:** Request transfer of your data
- **Object:** Object to data processing
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for data processing

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights by managing app data through iOS Settings or uninstalling the app.

For inquiries about crash reports or other data, contact us at intelhelper@daviesgeek.com.

### All Users

Regardless of your location, you can:

- Disable location access: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Intel Helper
- Limit ad tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
- Delete all app data: Uninstall the app from your device
